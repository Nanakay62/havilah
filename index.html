How it works

Three straightforward steps to establish confidential, measured wellbeing in your organization.

1

HR invites employees

Administrators set up departments and send secure onboarding links or email invites to employees.

2

Employees complete confidential check-ins

Employees complete validated check-ins from any device. Individual answers are strictly private.

3

HR sees anonymous group results and can refer employees to a medical assessor

Leadership monitors group-level wellbeing indicators and employees can access confidential clinical consultations.

Frequently asked questions

Common questions about confidentiality, data protection, and platform billing.

Who can see my answers?
Individual responses are strictly confidential. HR administrators and employers only see aggregated group metrics when at least 5 employees have completed an assessment. Individual screening answers are never revealed.
Where is data stored?
All organizational and wellbeing data is stored in secure regional data facilities with strict encryption in transit and at rest, designed with Ghana Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) principles in mind.

Privacy Policy

1. Data Protection Principles

Havilah is designed with the Ghana Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) in mind. Built with the principles of the Act in mind: data minimisation, purpose limitation and security.

2. What We Collect

We collect organization contact details (name, work email, organization name), employee assessment responses (validated psychometric screening scores), and voluntary clinical referral requests initiated by employees.

3. Why We Collect It

Data is processed solely to provide confidential personal wellbeing evaluations for employees and anonymized, aggregated psychosocial risk indicators for organizational leaders.

4. Anonymity Threshold

HR administrators and managers are never shown individual survey responses. Aggregate results are only displayed for groups or departments of 5 or more respondents. If fewer than 5 responses exist for a segment, data is withheld.

5. Data Retention

Organizational check-in data is retained for the duration of the active subscription agreement. Upon account termination or contract expiry, data is archived or deleted upon request when a subscription ends.

6. Contacting the Privacy Officer

For inquiries or data subject requests, please contact our privacy desk at nanakwamedickson553@gmail.com.
