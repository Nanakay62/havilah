How your privacy works

HR only sees combined scores for a group or department when at least five people have responded. They cannot see your individual answers, personal scores, or when you submitted a check-in.
Only the licensed practitioner at FZ Safety and Health Centre sees your name, contact details, and reason for reaching out. Your employer does not see who requested a consultation or what you discussed.
A daily check-in is a quick log to help you track your own mood, calm, stress, energy, and work balance over time. A formal assessment is a structured questionnaire, such as PHQ-9, PSS-10, or COPSOQ, opened by your workplace during a scheduled survey period.
Formal assessments open only when your organization starts a scheduled survey cycle. Once you submit an assessment for that cycle, it locks until the next survey period begins.
Your overall index is a score from 0 to 100 calculated from your recent check-ins and completed assessments across mood, calm, stress, energy, and work balance. A higher score means healthier balance, while a lower score highlights areas of strain.
Yes, daily check-ins are completely optional and you can use them at your own pace. Logging them simply gives you a clearer picture of your personal trends over time.
