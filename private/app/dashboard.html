How Your Data is Protected

Havilah is engineered under the ISO 45003 standard for Psychological Health & Safety at Work. Your individual survey responses are encrypted and stored in your private employee vault.

  • 🔒
    Individual Responses are Vaulted: Your manager, HR team, or employer can NEVER see individual check-ins, scores, or timestamps.
  • 📊
    N ≥ 5 Minimum Anonymity Threshold: Aggregated reports are only generated when at least 5 employees in a group complete assessments. Smaller groups are automatically merged.
  • ✍️
    Voluntary & Non-Coercive: All check-ins are optional. Consent can be updated or revoked in your Privacy Settings at any time.

What is the difference between Daily Pulse and Formal Surveys?

Daily Pulse is a 2-minute micro self-check across 5 dimensions. Formal surveys (COPSOQ III, PHQ-9, PSS-10) are validated clinical instruments unlocked by HR during scheduled assessment cycles.

Why are formal assessment cards locked?

Formal surveys are unlocked by HR during specific evaluation cycles to provide targeted organizational baseline measurements.

How is my Overall Index computed?

Your index is normalized on a 0–100 scale across Mood, Calm, Stress, Energy, and Work-Fit. Higher scores indicate optimal balance.
