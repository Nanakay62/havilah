Platform Capabilities

Everything your people need.
Nothing they don't.

A unified workspace for clinical-grade assessments, organizational insight, and confidential self-care - built on validated psychometric instruments.

Validated instruments

PHQ-9, GAD-7, PSS-10, FAS-10, and the full COPSOQ-II battery - administered and scored to clinical standards.

Multi-tenant architecture

Department, role, and location segmentation with role-based access control. Your data, partitioned by design.

Privacy-first by design

Individual responses are encrypted and never exposed. HR sees aggregated distributions only - never individual scores.

Predictive heatmaps

Spot burnout hotspots before they become turnover. Spatial risk mapping across teams, roles, and time windows.

Pulse cadencing

Schedule quarterly deep assessments or weekly pulse check-ins. Adaptive reminders respect each employee's rhythm.

Care pathway routing

When results indicate risk, the platform privately surfaces EAP resources, counseling, or occupational health referrals.
